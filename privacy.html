Privacy Policy

Mindorb · Last updated 28 July 2026

Mindorb is a place to put what's on your mind. That means it holds genuinely personal material, so here is a plain account of what is stored, where it goes, and what you can do about it — no boilerplate.

The short version

  • If you never sign in, everything stays on your device.
  • Signing in syncs your thoughts so you can use more than one device.
  • Your thoughts are never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train anyone's AI models.
  • Text you capture is sent to an AI provider to be organised, and is not retained by them for training.
  • You can export everything, and you can delete your account and its data outright.

Where your words go when the AI reads them

Mindorb's organising, unpacking, recall, photo reading and voice transcription are performed by Google's Gemini API. To do that, the relevant content is sent to Google:

  • Text you capture — to work out what it is about and where it belongs.
  • Voice recordings — to transcribe what you said. The audio is sent for transcription and is not kept by us afterwards.
  • Photos of notes — only ones you deliberately take or choose in the import screen, and only to read the text on them. We do not store the image.
  • A summary index of your thoughts — when you ask a question, so the answer can be based on what you actually wrote.

Google's paid API terms state that this data is not used to train their models. We do not keep copies of anything sent for processing beyond what is needed to return the result to you.

If you would rather nothing left your device: don't sign in, and use the typing option instead of voice. Capture and organisation still need the AI, so if that matters to you, be mindful of what you put in.

Sharing a bubble

There are two ways to send one, and they store different things.

  • A link saves a snapshot of the bubble and everything inside it, with a random address. Anyone holding that link can open it once they are signed in. The link is unguessable, but it is not a password, so treat it like any link you send.
  • Straight to someone in the app saves the same snapshot addressed to that person. It sits in their inbox until they add it or discard it, and discarding deletes it.
  • Either way it is a snapshot: editing the original afterwards does not change what you already sent.
  • Anyone can send you a bubble by username. If you would rather they did not, blocking them stops it, and that is enforced by the database rather than the interface.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We show no advertising and share nothing with advertisers.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking software, and we do not build a profile of you.
  • We do not read your thoughts except where you have explicitly asked the AI to act on them.

Your control

  • Export — Settings → Export Your Data gives you the whole lot as a file.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account. Everything other people can see (shared bubbles, friends, your leaderboard score) is removed immediately, and you are signed out and this device is wiped. Your own synced thoughts and your username are held for 7 days so a mistaken tap can be undone, then permanently deleted. Nobody else can take your username during that window. Signing in during those 7 days offers to restore the account.
  • Delete a thought — Delete moves it to Old Bubbles; Delete Forever there removes it for good.
  • Use it signed out — nothing is synced at all, and your thoughts stay on your device.

Deleting your account removes your synced thoughts, profile, username, friends, inbox and leaderboard score. Shared links you created stop working straight away. Copies that other people already added to their own galaxies are theirs and remain with them, and feedback or support messages you sent us are kept so we can still answer them.

Children

Mindorb is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Synced data is protected by row-level security rules so that your thoughts are readable only by your own account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and never visible to us. No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects what happens to your data, the date at the top will change and the app will say so.

Contact

Use Settings → Send Feedback in the app, which reaches us directly, for any question about your data or this policy.
